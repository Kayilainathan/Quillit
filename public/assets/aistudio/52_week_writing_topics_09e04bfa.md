# 52-Week Writing & Communication Curriculum (156 Topics)

A minimal, year-long roadmap of **3 topics per week** (no fixed days or dates) built for an Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering (EIE) undergraduate to build fluency across **technical documentation, reports, emails, LinkedIn posts, spoken interviews, everyday English, and creative storytelling**.

## How to Use This List

- **Pace:** Pick any 3 days each week and work through the 3 topics in order (`.1` Technical/Report/Speech, `.2` Email/LinkedIn/General Comm, `.3` Creative/Storytelling).
- **Universal Pivot Rule:** If a technical concept or scenario feels unfamiliar, swap it immediately for any lab, project, gadget, or real-life situation you know well. Focus on how cleanly your words flow.
- **Registers Covered:** `Casual` (spoken flow, dialogue, stories), `Semi-Formal` (LinkedIn, peer updates, interviews), `Formal` (cold emails, escalations, proposals), and `Report` (incident logs, test summaries, technical specs).
